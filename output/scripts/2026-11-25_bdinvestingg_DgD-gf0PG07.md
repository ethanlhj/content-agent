# Script: Stop running from the hard part....

**Date:** 2026-11-25
**Source:** [@bdinvestingg](https://www.instagram.com/p/DgD-gf0PG07/)
**Format to reuse:** Mindset provocation: open with a counterintuitive emotional stance, stack 3-4 short commands that reframe discomfort as advantage, close with a direct challenge or invitation to stay engaged.
**Hook pattern:** Shut up and let me enjoy [uncomfortable thing].
**Why the original worked:** It weaponizes vulnerability as strength, making the audience feel the content is a dare rather than a lesson.

---

## HOOK
Stop running from the hard part.

## BODY
1. Most people quit the moment investing gets uncomfortable. Portfolio down 20%? They sell. That discomfort is information, not a stop sign.

2. The investors who build real wealth sit in the ugly. They watch red days, hold the position, and do not flinch. That tolerance is a skill you train.

3. Not financial advice, but what I watch is which stocks I can hold through pain without panic. If you cannot, you do not actually believe in the thesis.
